# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

BYO3 speaks to Italian adults, mostly 35-65, who care about daily wellbeing and are skeptical of exaggerated supplement claims. They compare ingredients, want clear science in plain language, and prefer a premium Italian brand that explains before it sells.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces BYO3 as a premium Italian nutraceutical brand built around OZOILAB, ozonized and stabilized sunflower oil in daily capsules. The homepage must educate quickly, guide visitors toward the right product, and prepare the store for a later Shopify backend without overpromising benefits.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, calm, precise. The voice is authoritative but accessible, premium without distance, and honest about gradual results. It should look like science and speak like a human.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a generic supplement store, a cold medical clinic, an aggressive biohacking brand, a detox lifestyle page, or a stock wellness template. Avoid clinical imagery, miracle claims, hype language, neon gradients, fake urgency, and all copy that suggests treatment or cure.

## Design Principles

Lead with clarity before conversion.

Use product imagery and simple visual explanations to make OZOILAB understandable.

Make the product family easy to scan by need: energia, uomo, donna, intestino, articolazioni.

Build trust through Italian research, transparent ingredients, careful warnings, and measured language.

Keep the homepage calm and premium while making the buying path obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from 375px through desktop. Avoid motion that blocks comprehension and never use color as the only indicator.
